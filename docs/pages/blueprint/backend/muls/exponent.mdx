# The Exponent Idea [Achiving unsigned integer multiplication by working in the exponent]

Here, we explain a fairly simple mathematical idea that underlies our multiplication technique.

We fix integers $p$ and $q$, in the range $\{0, \ldots , 2^{64} - 1\}$. We're interested in $p \cdot q$. Recall that we work throughout with binary fields. This means that we can't use typical prime-field ideas—say, that of breaking $p$ and $q$ into limbs so small that their products don't overflow the characteristic.
